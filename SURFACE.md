# Pale Specimen Ledger

Scope: a first-visit portfolio reading experience for recruiters, collaborators, and friends.

Visitor job: understand Kuan Yi Wang's work and interests, inspect selected projects, then contact him.

Proof and constraints: reuse the approved experience, project, CV, email, and social links from the existing portfolio; light, minimal, text-led; use HW4 handwriting when possible and JetBrains Mono for the remaining glyphs.

Chosen direction: a pale field ledger with graphite rules, hand-written marginalia, and a scroll-scrubbed dither llama study. The scroll controls the study forwards and backwards, leaving a clear replacement seam for a future Figma Weave export.

Unresolved: replace the native llama study with Kuan's final Figma Weave media when available.
