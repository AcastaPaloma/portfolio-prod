Every clip replays an attempt that was actually scored: the simulator reruns with the model's logged answers, and the outcome is checked against the log. Panels in one video share a clock and the same starting scene. When an attempt ends, its panel freezes under a green SUCCESS or red FAIL banner. Short attempts play at real speed, long ones at 2× (the title bar says which).
